Abstract

Semantic misinformation can arise when an authentic image is paired with a caption that changes or distorts its narrative. Because the image itself may contain no visible signs of manipulation, methods designed to detect pixel-level artifacts can miss these misleading image-caption pairs. We study this problem using DGM4+, a dataset designed to evaluate the detection of global scene and semantic inconsistencies. We propose a multimodal model that combines image-text representations from SigLIP with visual scene features from DINOv2 through a projection MLP, and adapts Qwen2-VL-7B using LoRA. The model jointly predicts whether an image-caption pair is authentic, identifies the manipulation type, and grounds the misleading caption tokens. On the DGM4+ validation split, the model achieves an F1 score of $0.9679$ for binary authenticity classification. These results suggest that parameter-efficient adaptation of vision-language models is promising for detecting semantic inconsistencies, while also motivating further work on precise token-level grounding.

Motivation

语义虚假信息可由真实图像与扭曲叙事的字幕配对产生，图像本身无像素级篡改痕迹，传统检测像素伪影的方法会漏检此类误导性图文对。

Method

提出多模态模型：通过投影MLP融合SigLIP的图像-文本表示与DINOv2的视觉场景特征，并使用LoRA对Qwen2-VL-7B进行参数高效适配；模型联合预测图文对真实性、识别操纵类型并定位误导性字幕词元。

Result

在DGM4+验证集上，二分类真实性分类的F1分数达到0.9679。

Conclusion

视觉语言模型的参数高效适配在检测语义不一致方面具有前景，同时仍需进一步研究精确的词元级定位。

Abstract

Objective: To develop a unified vision-language model for prompt-conditioned quantitative ultrasound measurement that addresses the mismatch between continuous measurement targets and discrete token-based numerical prediction. Methods: This article presents HAD-VLM, a hybrid autoregressive-diffusion framework that combines autoregressive text generation with continuous measurement prediction within a shared backbone. Visual prompt tuning adapts the vision encoder to ultrasound images. A diffusion process predicts a five-component representation comprising caliper endpoint coordinates and measurement length. A post-processing strategy combining the proposed coordinate-length consistency mechanism with ensemble prediction is introduced to refine measurement estimates. We evaluate four echocardiographic measurement tasks on EchoNet-LVH, with additional assessments of medical question answering and X-ray vertebral measurement. Results: HAD-VLM achieves mean absolute errors of 1.36, 2.77, 3.05, and 1.16 mm for IVSd, LVIDd, LVIDs, and LVPWd, respectively, reducing errors by 21.4-37.6\% relative to the Vicuna-based autoregressive baseline. It also outperforms fine-tuned Qwen3-VL and direct MLP regression across all four tasks. Medical question answering performance is preserved on the evaluated dataset. For X-ray vertebral measurement, HAD-VLM reduces mean absolute error from 11.4 to 6.7 pixels. Conclusion: Integrating autoregressive language generation with diffusion-based continuous prediction improves quantitative medical image measurement while retaining prompt-conditioned task flexibility. Significance: HAD-VLM provides a unified approach to combining language interaction with anatomically grounded numerical prediction for quantitative medical image analysis.

Abstract

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models inherit rich semantic representations from pretrained Vision-Language Models, yet fine-tuning on limited robot demonstrations degrades this structure and undermines generalization. A fundamental question therefore arises: what constitutes a good action representation? Inspired by the mirror neuron theory's insight that observation and execution share an intention-level encoding, we examine whether a robot's action representations preserve the semantic structure captured by pretrained encoders. Systematic probing confirms that this structure erodes during finetuning, and that its quality synchronizes with both task success and out-of-distribution generalization. We further introduce a plug-and-play method that anchors action representations to a semantic manifold while decomposing representations into a shared semantic channel and a private channel, all discarded at inference, leaving the deployed model unchanged. Validated on different VLA backbones across simulation and real-world benchmarks, our method yields up to +18.7% on real-world in-distribution tasks and +21.5% on out-of-distribution generalization.

Motivation

系统探测确认微调中语义结构侵蚀且其质量与任务成功和OOD泛化同步；提出即插即用方法，将动作表征锚定到语义流形，并分解为共享语义通道与私有通道，推理时全部丢弃，部署模型不变。

在不同VLA骨干、仿真与真实世界基准上验证，真实世界分布内任务最高提升+18.7%，分布外泛化最高提升+21.5%。

Conclusion

Abstract

Long egocentric videos pose a central challenge for multimodal language models: the visual stream must be compressed into a memory representation that can be searched efficiently while still preserving the evidence needed for question answering. Building on an online textual-memory framework in which first-person videos are converted into temporally grounded captions, this work systematically studies the role of retrieval in episodic-memory video question answering. We evaluate how different retrieval units, query formulations, context budgets, and reranking strategies affect multiple-choice answering on QAEgo4D-Closed, isolating retrieval as the main variable while keeping the underlying generated memory fixed. Our best configuration, Parent, matches fine-grained caption subchunks, then provides their full parent captions to the reasoner. It achieves 56.4 % accuracy, matching strong full-memory and retrieval baselines while exposing only a small fraction of the textual memory at question time. More importantly, our analysis shows that better temporal coverage does not necessarily translate into better answers: increasing the retrieval budget improves temporal Hit@K and Recall@K, but can reduce accuracy by introducing visually plausible distractors and uncertain caption evidence. These findings suggest that retrieval-augmented egocentric memory should not be optimized only for evidence recall, but also for the reliability, specificity, and answer-discriminative value of the retrieved context. The study motivates more structured episodic memories that move beyond raw caption retrieval toward evidence-aware representations for long-form egocentric reasoning.

Motivation

长第一人称视频对多模态语言模型构成挑战：视觉流需压缩为可高效搜索的记忆表示，同时保留问答所需证据。现有在线文本记忆框架将视频转为时序字幕，但检索在情景记忆视频问答中的作用尚未被系统研究。

Method

最佳配置Parent达到56.4%准确率，匹配强全记忆和检索基线，同时仅暴露小部分文本记忆。分析表明，增加检索预算提升时间Hit@K和Recall@K，但可能因引入视觉上合理的干扰项和不确定字幕证据而降低准确率。

Abstract

Unlike question answering, where a query specifies a perceptual target, open-ended detailed captioning must determine what evidence to acquire and how to integrate it into a coherent global description. Abstract

While multimodal contrastive models like CLIP and CLAP successfully learn shared representations, their embeddings typically exhibit a persistent \emph{modality gap}. We propose addressing this separation through a purely geometric approach: changing the embedding domain from the unit sphere, $S^{d-1}$, to the probability simplex, $Δ^{d-1}$. Although both manifolds share the same intrinsic dimension, the simplex acts as a structural prior; its nonnegative, unit-mass constraints serve as a cross-modal calibration that forces both modalities to ground themselves in the same coordinate system. By attaching lightweight softmax adapters and using Total Variation similarity, we reduce the centroid $\ell_2$ gap by 97--99\% across five multimodal retrieval benchmarks. This confirms that simplicial geometry induces genuine semantic alignment, matching or exceeding the retrieval performance of standard unconstrained models.

Motivation

CLIP、CLAP等多模态对比模型虽能学习共享表示，但其嵌入存在持续的模态间隙，即不同模态的表示在嵌入空间中彼此分离，影响跨模态对齐。

Method

将嵌入域从单位球面S^{d-1}改为概率单纯形Δ^{d-1}，利用其非负、单位质量约束作为跨模态校准的结构先验；附加轻量级softmax适配器，并采用总变差相似度进行对齐。

Result

Abstract

Geographic regions are multimodal: satellite imagery captures visual morphology, text conveys human-interpretable semantics, and map structures describe urban context. Existing geospatial contrastive methods typically align image-text or image-location pairs, underusing structured map data. We introduce GeoLEON-CLIP, a graph-anchored contrastive framework that aligns three views of each H3-indexed region: an OpenStreetMap-derived graph embedding, a satellite image crop, and a textual description. Frozen pretrained LEON graph embeddings provide a semantic anchor between vision and language. Frozen graph, image, and text encoders are connected by trainable residual projection heads and trained with a symmetric tri-modal contrastive objective. Experiments show strong graph-image and graph-text alignment; graph-image retrieval achieves the best cross-modal performance in a mixed-resolution pool. To evaluate the anchoring hypothesis, we propose chained cross-modal recall $\mathrm{R}@A@B$, which routes retrieval through the graph modality and compares it with direct retrieval under a matched candidate budget. At $\mathrm{R}@1@1$, image$\rightarrow$graph$\rightarrow$text and text$\rightarrow$graph$\rightarrow$image outperform direct image-text retrieval by 4.8 and 4.4 points, respectively, despite using a single intermediate cell. Downstream probes show task-dependent gains, with the largest improvements on Airbnb and Beijing housing. Separating Angular Alignment from Radial Ordering in Hyperbolic Vision-Language Models

Abstract

Hyperbolic vision-language models are well suited to modeling the hierarchical structure of multimodal data, because hyperbolic volume grows exponentially with radius, enabling low-distortion representations of tree-like taxonomies from general to specific concepts. However, existing pretraining objectives for hyperbolic vision-language models entangle semantic alignment with entailment modeling, forcing radial coordinates to simultaneously encode semantic matching and hierarchical structure, leading to conflicting geometric constraints. In this paper, we introduce AURORA, a hyperbolic vision-language framework that decouples the objectives through angular alignment and a novel Radial-Angular Order Cone (RAOC) for entailment modeling. Angular alignment captures semantic similarity while preserving the radial structure for representing hierarchies. Unlike conventional entailment cones, RAOC determines angular allowance from relative parent-child radial gaps. It is incorporated into hierarchical entailment contrastive training to capture partial-order relations. We further introduce a norm regularization term that prevents undesirable radial degeneration for stable training. We train models on millions of image-text pairs and evaluate them across zero-shot classification, image-text retrieval, hierarchical reasoning, and compositional understanding. Our model consistently outperforms strong Euclidean and hyperbolic vision-language baselines, demonstrating the effectiveness of decoupling semantic alignment from hierarchical entailment modeling.

Motivation

现有双曲视觉-语言预训练目标将语义对齐与蕴含建模纠缠，迫使径向坐标同时编码语义匹配和层次结构，导致几何约束冲突。

Method

AURORA框架：角度对齐捕捉语义相似性并保留径向结构表示层次；提出RAOC，根据父子径向差距确定角度允许范围，并融入层次蕴含对比训练以捕捉偏序关系；引入范数正则项防止径向退化。

Result

在数百万图像-文本对上训练，零样本分类、图像-文本检索、层次推理和组合理解任务上一致优于强欧氏和双曲视觉-语言基线。

Conclusion

解耦语义对齐与层次蕴含建模是有效的，AURORA框架验证了该方法的优越性。
