Abstract

A panoramic video captures all directions around the camera, but relevant evidence can appear in different directions and at different times. Understanding it requires models to connect observations across the viewing sphere and time. We introduce PanoVideoBench, the first manually annotated benchmark for comprehensive panoramic video understanding. Its 1,000 questions span five categories and 15 question types, with diagnostic annotations for target search and panoramic capabilities. Evaluation of 11 proprietary, open-source, and panoramic-specialized models identifies cross-view tracking and sphere-time evidence integration as their weakest capabilities. To strengthen these capabilities, we construct PanoVideoCoT, the first reasoning dataset grounded in real panoramic video, through sphere-time-grounded synthesis that converts verified panoramic observations and successful agentic explorations into 13,465 QA-CoT pairs. Starting from a general video MLLM, PanoVideoReasoner learns from this corpus through Panoramic CoT SFT and Evidence-Privileged On-Policy Self-Distillation, a panoramic instantiation of Vision-OPD's OPSD in which the teacher uses verified perspective views to guide student-generated trajectories. It reaches 55.7% on PanoVideoBench, improving the backbone by 12.5 points and the previous SOTA by 2.6. The gains concentrate on the same spatial, cross-view, and temporal capabilities exposed by the benchmark, showing that grounded sphere-time supervision can equip a general video MLLM with panoramic reasoning.

Motivation

全景视频覆盖相机周围所有方向，相关证据可能出现在不同方向和不同时间，现有模型难以跨视角球面与时间连接观测；缺乏全面评估全景视频理解的基准，且跨视角跟踪与球面-时间证据整合是模型最弱能力。

Method

构建PanoVideoBench（1000题、5类15种题型、含目标搜索与全景能力诊断标注）；通过球面-时间 grounded 合成，将验证过的全景观测与成功的智能体探索转化为13,465个QA-CoT对，构建PanoVideoCoT；从通用视频MLLM出发，采用全景CoT监督微调与证据特权在线自蒸馏（Vision-OPD中OPSD的全景实例化，教师用验证过的透视视图引导学生生成轨迹）训练PanoVideoReasoner。

Result

PanoVideoReasoner在PanoVideoBench上达到55.7%，比骨干模型提升12.5个百分点，比此前SOTA提升2.6个百分点；增益集中在基准所暴露的空间、跨视角和时间能力上。

Conclusion

基于球面-时间 grounded 的监督可使通用视频MLLM获得全景推理能力，验证了该训练范式的有效性。

Abstract

Multimodal Chain-of-Thought (MCoT) substantially enhances the complex reasoning capabilities of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) by explicitly modeling intermediate reasoning processes. However, most learning-based MCoT methods follow a stage-centric paradigm, where different reasoning behaviors are assigned to predefined stages and optimized separately. This results in fragmented reasoning and a training-inference reasoning-state gap. To address these limitations, we propose ReState, a unified iterative reasoning framework that reformulates multimodal reasoning from a stage-driven pipeline into a state-driven dynamic process. It comprises two complementary components: Unified Reasoning State Modeling (URSM) and Closed-Loop Reasoning-State Learning (CRSL). URSM unifies reasoning construction, correct reasoning preservation, and erroneous reasoning correction as reasoning-state transitions within a shared state space, enabling these heterogeneous reasoning behaviors to be learned jointly. CRSL further incorporates self-generated historical reasoning states during training and recursively updates the current state during inference, thereby mitigating the reasoning-state gap and enabling iterative self-refinement without requiring an additional reflection module, critic, or independent correction stage. Furthermore, we construct VersaCoT-10K, a high-quality CoT dataset spanning eight visual reasoning domains, which provides diverse supervision for multi-domain reasoning and reasoning-state refinement. Extensive experiments demonstrate that ReState exhibits strong adaptability across different MLLM backbones and achieves strong performance on multiple public reasoning benchmarks, supporting the shift from stage-centric reasoning toward state-centric reasoning. Our data and code are available at https://restate-project.github.io

Motivation

现有基于学习的多模态思维链方法多采用阶段中心范式，将不同推理行为分配给预定义阶段并分别优化，导致推理碎片化以及训练与推理之间的推理状态差距。

Method

提出ReState框架，包含统一推理状态建模（URSM）和闭环推理状态学习（CRSL）。URSM将推理构建、正确推理保持和错误推理纠正统一为共享状态空间中的状态转移，实现联合学习；CRSL在训练中引入自生成历史推理状态，推理时递归更新当前状态，实现迭代自精炼，无需额外反思模块、评论器或独立纠正阶段。同时构建VersaCoT-10K数据集，覆盖八个视觉推理领域。

Result

大量实验表明，ReState在不同MLLM骨干网络上具有强适应性，并在多个公开推理基准上取得强劲性能。

Conclusion

ReState支持从阶段中心推理向状态中心推理的转变，有效缓解推理碎片化和训练-推理状态差距，实现无需额外模块的迭代自精炼。

Abstract

A capable surgical AI model should reason from visual evidence; yet, many existing surgical benchmarks can be partially solved without visual input, as models may exploit procedural priors or common anatomy-instrument interactions to infer answers from language alone. To address this, we introduce SUREON, a video question-answering (QA) dataset and benchmark designed to evaluate visually grounded surgical reasoning. Our pipeline generates QA pairs from narrated surgical academic videos, refines incorrect options using multiple models to reduce linguistic biases, and uses trained adversarial guessers to filter out questions answerable without visual evidence. The resulting dataset contains 76K training pairs covering 168 procedures and 10 specialties, alongside a benchmark of 340 questions across 10 categories verified by expert surgeons. Benchmarking leading commercial and open-source models shows that surgical visual reasoning remains largely unsolved--no model exceeds 53% accuracy and even expert surgeons reach only 64.9%. We also introduce SUREON-VLM, a baseline trained on our curated data that avoids language shortcuts, outperforms existing open-source models on SUREON, and matches larger specialist models on multiple standard surgical benchmarks. Our training ablations show that distractor refinement and blind curation strengthen visual grounding, increasing the multimodal gain from 8.7 to 29.1 percentage points while reducing the training set from 176.6K to 76K filtered samples. SUREON thus offers both a verified testbed for surgical visual reasoning and training data that strengthens visual grounding.

Motivation

现有手术AI基准可部分不依赖视觉输入而通过语言先验或解剖-器械交互常识解答，无法真正评估视觉基础推理能力。

Method

从 narrated 手术学术视频生成QA对，用多模型精炼错误选项以减少语言偏差，训练对抗猜测器过滤无需视觉证据即可回答的问题；构建76K训练对（168种手术、10个专科）和340题专家验证基准（10类）；训练SUREON-VLM基线。

Result

领先商业和开源模型准确率均不超53%，专家外科医生仅64.9%；SUREON-VLM避免语言捷径，在SUREON上优于现有开源模型，在多个标准手术基准上匹配更大专科模型；消融显示干扰项精炼和盲策展将多模态增益从8.7提升至29.1个百分点，训练集从176.6K降至76K。

Conclusion

SUREON提供了经验证的手术视觉推理测试平台和增强视觉基础的训练数据，表明手术视觉推理仍远未解决。

Ankit Khedia, Astitva Jaiswal, Chaitanya Prasad, Anisha Raju, Aangeeras

Abstract

Video captioning models are increasingly evaluated with metrics designed for single-sentence image captions. We test whether those metrics recover human judgement on dense, multi-window video description, and find that they do not. Six frontier models captioned 196 short clips. Trained annotators post-edited every caption against a failure taxonomy fixed before annotation, once as a whole-clip overview and once window by window, and a quality-control reviewer checked each annotation, yielding 1,144 overview verdicts and 4,672 window verdicts. Reference-overlap metrics do not separate the captions reviewers accepted from those they rejected: BLEU-4 reaches an AUC of 0.510 and a CIDEr-style tf-idf cosine 0.528, and restricting references to reviewer corrections leaves BLEU-4 at 0.479 (95% CI 0.431-0.529), indistinguishable from chance. Review granularity changes what is detected. The same annotators record camera and shot errors on 2.7% of captions when reading the overview and on 12.8% when reading window by window, a 4.7x gap that holds across every robustness check we ran, on the mode most likely to need a near-total rewrite. Correct-overview rates span 54.1-72.7%, but only 4 of 15 pairwise gaps survive Bonferroni correction (5 after adjusting for reviewer and clip effects), and accuracy and derived severity order the models differently. Models with statistically indistinguishable accuracy have sharply different failure profiles, which no single scalar shows. We argue that caption evaluation should record what was wrong rather than only how often, and we report the taxonomy, the per-model profiles, the corpus properties that predict difficulty, and the code that regenerates every number in the paper.

Motivation

视频描述模型越来越多地使用为单句图像描述设计的指标进行评估，但这些指标是否适用于密集、多窗口视频描述任务、能否反映人类判断尚不清楚。论文旨在检验现有指标在视频描述评估中的有效性，并揭示其局限性。

Method

六种前沿模型为196个短视频片段生成描述。训练有素的标注者依据预先固定的失败分类法对每个描述进行后编辑，分别以整体片段概览和逐窗口两种粒度进行标注，并由质量控制审核员检查每份标注，最终获得1,144条概览判定和4,672条窗口判定。通过计算BLEU-4、CIDEr风格tf-idf余弦等参考重叠指标的AUC，比较评审接受与拒绝的描述，并分析评审粒度对错误检测的影响，以及模型间准确率差异的统计显著性（Bonferroni校正）。

Result

参考重叠指标无法区分评审接受与拒绝的描述：BLEU-4的AUC为0.510，CIDEr风格tf-idf余弦为0.528；仅使用评审修正作为参考时BLEU-4为0.479（95% CI 0.431-0.529），与随机无异。评审粒度影响检测结果：同一批标注者在整体概览下记录相机和镜头错误的比例为2.7%，逐窗口下为12.8%，相差4.7倍，且在所有稳健性检验中均成立。正确概览率在54.1%-72.7%之间，但15对模型比较中仅4对（调整评审和片段效应后5对）通过Bonferroni校正；准确率与派生严重度对模型的排序不同。统计上准确率无显著差异的模型具有截然不同的失败特征，单一标量无法体现。

Conclusion

视频描述评估不应仅依赖为单句图像描述设计的参考重叠指标，而应记录错误类型（what was wrong）而非仅统计错误频率（how often）。论文发布了失败分类法、各模型失败特征、预测难度的语料属性以及可复现所有数字的代码。

Abstract

Video captioning aims to preserve visual information through compact descriptions, predominantly using natural language. However, precisely expressing fine-grained spatial structures and temporal dynamics in natural language often leads to lengthy and potentially ambiguous descriptions. In this work, we explore Code as Video Caption, a paradigm where executable programs serve as a complementary representation of visual content through graphical primitives, layout rules, and functions of time. To examine whether code captions effectively preserve video information, we compare them with natural language captions on downstream question answering and video reconstruction tasks. Our results show that code captions achieve superior reasoning accuracy and reconstruction fidelity. Despite these advantages, realizing the potential of code captioning depends on whether current multimodal large language models (MLLMs) can generate faithful code captions. To systematically assess this capability, we introduce CodeCap-Bench, which comprises 323 videos spanning five categories of structured 2D content, along with fine-grained annotations and a multi-level evaluation framework. Extensive experiments reveal that while leading proprietary MLLMs demonstrate promising code captioning capabilities, they still struggle to faithfully capture complex temporal dynamics, with open-source models lagging substantially behind.

Motivation

自然语言在精确表达视频中的细粒度空间结构和时间动态时往往导致描述冗长且可能产生歧义，因此需要一种更紧凑、无歧义的互补表示来保留视觉信息。

Method

提出“代码作为视频字幕”范式，用可执行程序（图形基元、布局规则、时间函数）表示视频内容；在问答和视频重建任务上与自然语言字幕对比；构建CodeCap-Bench基准（323个视频、五类结构化2D内容、细粒度标注和多级评估框架）系统评估MLLMs生成忠实代码字幕的能力。

Result

代码字幕在推理准确性和重建保真度上优于自然语言字幕；领先的专有MLLMs展现出有前景的代码字幕能力，但仍难以忠实捕捉复杂时间动态，开源模型显著落后。

Conclusion

代码字幕能更有效地保留视频信息，但其潜力取决于MLLMs能否生成忠实的代码字幕；当前模型在复杂时间动态建模上仍有明显不足，需进一步研究提升。

Abstract

Marine video understanding is crucial for a wide range of real-world applications. However, existing marine video datasets suffer from limitations, including the limited number of data samples, fine-grained text descriptions, and detailed visual attributes. To address these limitations, we introduce a large-scale, video-text dataset featuring rich, in-the-wild visual attributes tailored for complex marine environments. Our dataset comprises 2,000 video-text pairs annotated with 13 attributes covering up to four objects per scene, object visual/geometric properties, behaviors, environmental context, and camera motion, spanning diverse lighting conditions, times of day, and 7 countries. Abstract

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over visually rich documents must find both relevant content and the evidence that supports an answer. Screenshot retrieval preserves images and page structure, but written facts within screenshots also need to be searchable. Moreover, topical relevance alone does not identify which retrieved tile contains evidence for the answer. We introduce Pix2Evidence, a multimodal RAG system that encodes each screenshot tile together with its available transcription into a single fused image--text representation, so that layout and readable text are considered jointly during retrieval. To guide evidence selection, a vision-language teacher describes the visible content that supports each training question. We use these descriptions to adapt the retriever, then rerank the retrieved candidates before answer generation. Extensive experiments demonstrate that Pix2Evidence outperforms state-of-the-art methods. Across five widely-used Wikipedia QA benchmarks, the non-reranked Pix2Evidence leads the main comparison in answer accuracy on four. On MMSearch, it reaches 26.0\%, 4.4 percentage points above PixelRAG. We will release the code and share derived data where licensing and privacy reviews permit.

Motivation

在视觉丰富文档上的 RAG 需要同时找到相关内容和支持答案的证据；截图检索保留图像和页面结构，但截图中的文字事实也需可搜索；仅靠主题相关性无法确定哪个检索瓦片包含答案证据。

Method

将每个截图瓦片与其可用转录编码为单一融合的图像-文本表示，使布局和可读文本在检索中联合考虑；使用视觉语言教师模型描述支持每个训练问题的可见内容，用这些描述适配检索器，然后对检索候选重排序，再进行答案生成。

Result

在五个广泛使用的 Wikipedia QA 基准上，未重排序的 Pix2Evidence 在四个基准的答案准确率上领先主要对比；在 MMSearch 上达到 26.0%，比 PixelRAG 高 4.4 个百分点。

Conclusion

Pix2Evidence 通过融合图像-文本表示和证据引导的检索适配与重排序，有效提升了视觉丰富文档上的多模态 RAG 性能，并将发布代码和共享衍生数据（在许可和隐私审查允许的情况下）。

Abstract

Visual foundation models learn rich pathological morphology but lack explicit concept-level supervision. Vision--language pretraining can provide this supervision but requires costly image--text pairs. Here, we introduce GloSAGE, a semantic self-distillation framework that uses lesion profiles predicted by a pretrained visual teacher to construct structured pathology descriptions. Reliability-guided selection identifies image--text anchors, while Knowledge-Structured Pathology Text Synthesis and Evidence-Guided Negative-pair Construction account for partially observed lesions. Image--text alignment trains projection layers while retaining the frozen visual encoder; an intermediate Semantic Transfer Layer supplies features for downstream tasks. Developed on GloMillion and evaluated across seven glomerulus- and case-level cohorts, GloSAGE supported zero-shot lesion recognition, image--text retrieval, hard-negative discrimination and concept-intervention analyses. It outperformed its source visual model in most lesion-recognition tasks and improved lesion grading, while supporting competitive case-level disease classification, injury grading and baseline renal function estimation. Ablations supported the combined contributions of knowledge-structured text and evidence-guided negatives, and the intermediate transfer layer improved mean lesion-recognition ROC-AUC relative to the complete projection output. A frozen language model conditioned on GloSAGE features generated glomerular descriptions. These findings suggest that teacher-derived lesion profiles can provide annotation-efficient semantic supervision for extending a glomerular visual foundation model.

Motivation

视觉基础模型能学习丰富的病理形态但缺乏显式概念级监督，视觉-语言预训练可提供此类监督却依赖昂贵的图文对，因此需要一种标注高效的语义监督方法。

Method

提出GloSAGE语义自蒸馏框架：用预训练视觉教师预测的病变轮廓构建结构化病理描述；通过可靠性引导选择图文锚点；采用知识结构化病理文本合成和证据引导负样本构建处理部分观测病变；训练时冻结视觉编码器仅训练投影层，并引入中间语义迁移层为下游任务提供特征。

Result

在GloMillion上开发并在七个肾小球级和病例级队列评估，支持零样本病变识别、图文检索、难负样本判别和概念干预分析；在多数病变识别任务上优于源视觉模型并改善病变分级，病例级疾病分类、损伤分级和基线肾功能估计具竞争力；消融验证知识结构化文本与证据引导负样本的联合贡献，中间迁移层较完整投影输出提升平均病变识别ROC-AUC；冻结语言模型基于GloSAGE特征生成肾小球描述。

Conclusion

教师模型衍生的病变轮廓可为扩展肾小球视觉基础模型提供标注高效的语义监督。

Abstract

Remote sensing (RS) image-text retrieval plays a critical role in understanding massive RS imagery. However, the dense multi-object distribution and complex backgrounds in RS imagery make it difficult to simultaneously achieve fine-grained cross-modal alignment and efficient retrieval. Existing methods either rely on complex cross-modal interactions that lead to low retrieval efficiency, or depend on large-scale vision-language adaptation, which requires massive data and computational resources. To address these issues, we develop a fast-then-fine (FTF) retrieval framework centered on interaction-free multi-granular recall and parameter-free text-guided cross-modal reranking with the Balanced Text-guided Interaction Block (BTIB). Specifically, interaction-free multi-granular recall pre-computes gallery embeddings and matches them with the corresponding query embedding without full-gallery token-level text-image interaction, while text-guided cross-modal reranking with BTIB refines local semantic correspondence within the top-K candidate set. Furthermore, an inter- and intra-modal loss is designed to jointly optimize cross-modal alignment across multi-granular representations. Extensive experiments on public benchmarks demonstrate that the FTF achieves competitive retrieval accuracy while significantly improving retrieval efficiency compared with existing methods.

Motivation

遥感图像中密集的多目标分布和复杂背景使得细粒度跨模态对齐与高效检索难以兼得；现有方法要么因复杂跨模态交互导致检索效率低，要么依赖大规模视觉-语言适配，需要海量数据和计算资源。

Method

提出FTF框架，核心包括：无交互多粒度召回（预计算图库嵌入并与查询嵌入匹配，避免全图库token级文本-图像交互）和基于平衡文本引导交互块（BTIB）的参数无关文本引导跨模态重排序（在Top-K候选集内细化局部语义对应）；并设计模态间与模态内损失联合优化多粒度表示的跨模态对齐。

Result

在公开基准上的大量实验表明，FTF在取得有竞争力的检索精度的同时，相比现有方法显著提升了检索效率。

Conclusion

FTF框架通过无交互多粒度召回与参数无关文本引导重排序的结合，有效平衡了遥感图像-文本检索的细粒度对齐与检索效率，为大规模遥感图像理解提供了高效可行的方案。
