Sicheng Feng, Zigeng Chen, Song Wang, Gongfan Fang, Xinchao Wang

Abstract

Speculative decoding accelerates generation by letting a lightweight drafter propose tokens for parallel verification by a target model. Diffusion drafters such as DFlash propose an entire token block in a single forward pass, but mainly focus on text-only tasks, leaving multimodal drafting underexplored. To study this setting, we first introduce a metric of token-level visual dependence based on the KL divergence between next-token distributions with and without visual input. We find that acceptance rates decrease from 46% in the lowest visual-dependence quartile to 22% in the highest, with this disparity persisting after multimodal training. Motivated by this observation, we propose MMFlash, a simple and effective framework that adaptively upweights visually dependent tokens that the drafter predicts poorly. For long video inputs, MMFlash further employs sparse attention to reduce redundant attention computation and improve drafting quality. Across various multimodal benchmarks, MMFlash achieves lossless decoding speedups of up to $3.58\times$, outperforming EAGLE-3 and DFlash by up to 195.4% and 17.1%, respectively.

Motivation

现有扩散草稿器（如DFlash）主要面向纯文本任务，多模态草稿尚未充分探索；且视觉依赖度高的token接受率低，导致投机解码在多模态场景下效率受限。

Method

提出基于KL散度的token级视觉依赖度量；设计MMFlash框架，自适应上调草稿器预测较差的视觉依赖token权重；对长视频输入采用稀疏注意力减少冗余计算并提升草稿质量。

Result

在多模态基准上实现最高3.58倍无损解码加速，分别比EAGLE-3和DFlash最高提升195.4%和17.1%；接受率从最低视觉依赖四分位的46%降至最高四分位的22%，且多模态训练后差异仍存在。

Conclusion

视觉依赖是影响多模态投机解码接受率的关键因素，MMFlash通过针对性加权与稀疏注意力有效缓解该问题，显著提升多模态生成效率且保持无损。

Abstract

Multimodal continual instruction tuning requires multimodal large language models to sequentially acquire new capabilities without catastrophic forgetting. Mixture-of-experts (MoE) architectures offer a promising way to mitigate task interference through expert specialization and combination. However, while existing MoE-based methods avoid overwriting shared parameters by allocating separate experts for each task, this progressive expansion incurs severe cross-task redundancy and linear parameter growth. Specifically, experts across tasks often learn overlapping functions alongside task-specific capabilities. For instance, ScienceQA and TextVQA both locate relevant image regions. To curb parameter redundancy without inducing forgetting, we propose Consolidating and Preserving MoE (CoPre-MoE). For each incoming task, CoPre-MoE first learns task-specific MoE to capture specialized capabilities. To eliminate redundancy and reduce parameter growth across tasks, we design a shared-and-private expert architecture. Specifically, we first evaluate functional overlap to selectively consolidate overlapping experts across tasks into a fixed-capacity generic expert pool. We then factorize the remaining task-specific experts into shared projection bases and compact task-specific cores within a private expert pool. To mitigate forgetting, we construct the router by pairing each expert with learnable retrieval keys, where historical keys remain frozen during continual updates while newly added keys are regularized with an orthogonality penalty. At inference, input-dependent routing dynamically combines both pools without test-time task labels. Extensive experiments demonstrate that CoPre-MoE achieves superior performance while substantially reducing parameter overhead.

Motivation

现有基于MoE的持续指令微调方法为每个任务分配独立专家以避免覆盖共享参数，但导致严重的跨任务冗余和线性参数增长；不同任务的专家常学习重叠功能（如ScienceQA和TextVQA均需定位图像区域），因此需在避免遗忘的前提下抑制参数冗余。

Method

对每个新任务先学习任务特定MoE；设计共享-私有专家架构：评估功能重叠，将重叠专家选择性合并为固定容量通用专家池，并将剩余任务特定专家分解为共享投影基和紧凑任务特定核心存入私有专家池；路由器为每个专家配对可学习检索键，历史键在持续更新中冻结，新键施加正交性惩罚；推理时基于输入动态组合两个专家池，无需测试时任务标签。

Result

大量实验表明CoPre-MoE在取得更优性能的同时显著降低参数开销。

Conclusion

CoPre-MoE能有效缓解多模态持续指令微调中的灾难性遗忘，并通过共享-私有专家架构和检索键路由大幅减少跨任务参数冗余与增长。

Abstract

Vision-language-action (VLA) models adapt web-pretrained vision-language models to output robot actions, so that web knowledge carries over to physical control. In under 4 years the field has moved from RT-2, the first VLA, to open models that fine-tune on a single consumer GPU and to proprietary generalists that clean unseen homes. This survey reviews 88 works from December 2022 to October 2026, mainly on manipulation and navigation, plus humanoid control, world-action models and evaluation. We follow a VLA through its life cycle, from model design to training and deployment, along seven axes. Model design covers (a) inputs and memory, what the model observes; (b) architecture, how its pipeline is built; (c) action output, how it produces motor commands; and (d) intermediate predictions, what else it predicts, from reasoning steps to future observations. Training covers (e) pretraining data, the mix of robot, web, human and simulated data, and (f) post-training, adaptation to the target robot. Deployment covers (g) real-time execution, keeping pace with the robot. We analyse 22 models on these axes and report their training data, model size, inference speed and hardware. Three trends emerge: (i) training data has broadened, from about 10,000 hours of teleoperated robot data ($π_0$) to 23,000 community-collected episodes (SmolVLA) and 20,000 hours of egocentric human video (GR00T N1.7); (ii) model sizes have split: most recent models have 3-7 billion parameters, while a new group under one billion runs on consumer GPUs; and (iii) generalist VLAs are emerging that handle both manipulation and navigation, such as UniVLA and Qwen-VLA. We also compare VLAs with world-action models, which predict future observations as well as actions. We review evidence that some VLAs with near-perfect benchmark scores fail when objects or the camera move slightly, and open up further research questions.

Motivation

VLA模型将网络预训练的视觉-语言模型适配为输出机器人动作，使网络知识迁移至物理控制。该领域在不到4年内从首个VLA（RT-2）发展到可在单张消费级GPU上微调的开源模型及能清洁未见家庭的专有通用模型，亟需系统性综述以梳理进展、趋势与挑战。

Method

综述88项工作（2022年12月至2026年10月），主要覆盖操作与导航，兼及人形控制、世界动作模型和评估。沿VLA生命周期七轴分析：模型设计（输入与记忆、架构、动作输出、中间预测）、训练（预训练数据、后训练）、部署（实时执行）。对22个模型按上述轴分析，报告其训练数据、模型规模、推理速度和硬件，并与世界动作模型比较。

Result

三大趋势：(i) 训练数据拓宽，从约1万小时遥操作机器人数据（π0）到2.3万社区采集片段（SmolVLA）和2万小时自我中心人类视频（GR00T N1.7）；(ii) 模型规模分化，多数近期模型为30-70亿参数，另有一组低于10亿参数可在消费级GPU运行；(iii) 通用VLA涌现，如UniVLA和Qwen-VLA可同时处理操作与导航。此外，部分基准近满分VLA在物体或相机轻微移动时失效。

Conclusion

VLA领域快速发展，数据来源多样化、模型规模两极分化、通用化趋势明显；但基准性能与实际鲁棒性存在差距，轻微环境变化即可导致失败，由此提出进一步研究问题。

Abstract

Evaluating grammatical knowledge in language models requires benchmarks that cover diverse linguistic phenomena and are validated against human judgments. We introduce BLiMP-RO, a Romanian minimal-pair benchmark covering 50 grammatical phenomena across ten domains. It comprises 493 linguist-constructed sentence pairs, evaluated by native speakers in five experiments, and 5,942 additional semi-automatically generated, human-validated pairs. We further investigate grammatical generalization under limited linguistic input by training GPT-2 and GPT-BERT models from scratch on Romanian BabyBabelLM data and a newly collected corpus of 10 million words from child-targeted video captions. Increasing caption training data yields approximately 9 percentage points of accuracy improvement per tenfold increase in corpus size. At matched data sizes, BabyBabelLM generally outperforms video captions, while GPT-BERT consistently outperforms GPT-2. Our findings highlight how training data quantity, composition, and model architecture shape grammatical generalization in Romanian.

Motivation

评估语言模型的语法知识需要覆盖多样语言现象且经人类判断验证的基准，而罗马尼亚语缺乏此类资源；同时需探究有限语言输入下语法泛化的影响因素。

构建BLiMP-RO基准，包含493个语言学家构造的句子对（经母语者五项实验评估）和5,942个半自动生成且经人类验证的句子对，覆盖10个领域50种语法现象；从零训练GPT-2和GPT-BERT模型，使用罗马尼亚语BabyBabelLM数据和新收集的1000万词儿童目标视频字幕语料。

Keuntae Kim, Dongjun Kim, Yong Suk Choi

Published2026-11-13

Abstract

World-model video encoders such as V-JEPA learn how scenes change (direction, speed, order, state) from video alone. To answer questions, they are joined to a language model trained on captions and question-answer pairs. But each caption or question names only a few attributes of a clip, so the model learns to retrieve those attributes rather than to read the clip, and the dynamics the encoder carries go unused: on the same items, a linear probe reads speed from frozen V-JEPA features at 87% while the VLM answers at chance. We show where dynamics lives in a frozen encoder: in a subspace shared across videos for predictive encoders such as V-JEPA, but not as a shared component of frame-wise, text-aligned encoders such as SigLIP. We then build counterfactual supervision that makes the language model read it: pixel transformations turn public videos into answer-balanced variants whose answers are set by the transformation and differ only in the change. Trained in a single stage on 4.53M public samples with 32 visual tokens per frame, where open video LLMs use several stages, 9M to 172M samples and 64 to 256 tokens per frame on average, the resulting V-JEPA-LM models (1B to 8B, Qwen3 and Llama) lead every size class in average score across eleven video benchmarks; our 4B model scores 72.1, against 61.1 for the best open 4B and 64.8 for the best open 8B model. A controlled encoder x supervision study shows that the encoder decides what can be read and the supervision what is read: at a matched budget, counterfactual supervision adds 4.5 points on average and 38 on the speed questions of natural benchmarks. Code and models: https://github.com/kkt94/Llama-V-JEPA-LM.

Motivation

现有世界模型视频编码器（如V-JEPA）能学习场景变化，但与语言模型结合后，由于字幕和问答对仅命名少量属性，模型倾向于检索属性而非读取视频动态，导致编码器携带的动态信息未被利用（例如线性探针读取速度达87%，而VLM仅随机水平）。

Method

作者首先定位动态信息在冻结编码器中的位置：预测编码器（如V-JEPA）在跨视频共享子空间中编码动态，而逐帧文本对齐编码器（如SigLIP）则没有。然后构建反事实监督：通过像素变换将公开视频转化为答案平衡的变体，答案由变换决定且仅变化部分不同。在单阶段中使用4.53M公开样本、每帧32个视觉token训练V-JEPA-LM模型（1B至8B，基于Qwen3和Llama）。

Result

V-JEPA-LM在十一个视频基准的平均分上领先所有规模类别；4B模型得分72.1，优于最佳开放4B模型（61.1）和最佳开放8B模型（64.8）。控制实验表明，在匹配预算下，反事实监督平均提升4.5分，在自然基准的速度问题上提升38分。

Conclusion

编码器决定哪些信息可被读取，而监督信号决定实际读取什么；反事实监督能有效使语言模型利用V-JEPA编码器中的动态信息，且单阶段训练即可超越多阶段、大样本的开放视频LLM。

Abstract

Surgical planning is commonly evaluated by trajectory similarity to a single observed execution, a paradigm borrowed from activity anticipation benchmarks such as Ego4D. This conflates two distinct concepts: task completion and protocol adherence and assumes similarity to a completed surgical trajectory implies a valid plan. In surgery, this assumption is flawed: the clinically relevant target is protocol adherence rather than mere completion, and the reference trajectory is only one observed execution that may itself deviate from the protocol. We therefore introduce an evaluation framework grounded in expert-defined protocol rules that directly measures protocol adherence. Under this framework, similarity-based metrics systematically fail: they penalize protocol-adherent plans that deviate from the reference trajectory, while rewarding protocol-violating plans that remain superficially similar to it. Evaluating Video-LLMs further reveals two separable failure modes: unreliable video-to-state grounding and under-constrained planning. Neither is resolved by model scaling alone, whereas structural knowledge injection provides consistent gains. Our results motivate a shift from trajectory similarity toward protocol adherence as the evaluation standard for surgical planning.

Motivation

现有手术规划评估沿用Ego4D等活动预测基准的轨迹相似性范式，假设与单次观测执行轨迹相似即代表规划有效。但手术中临床相关目标是遵循协议而非单纯完成任务，参考轨迹本身可能偏离协议，因此需要直接衡量协议遵循的评估框架。

Method

提出基于专家定义协议规则的评估框架，直接测量手术规划对协议规则的遵循程度；在该框架下系统分析相似性指标的失效模式，并评估Video-LLM，区分视频到状态 grounding 不可靠与规划约束不足两种失败模式，同时检验模型缩放与结构知识注入的效果。

Result

相似性指标系统性失效：惩罚偏离参考轨迹但遵循协议的规划，奖励表面相似但违反协议的规划。Video-LLM评估揭示两种可分离失败模式：视频到状态 grounding 不可靠与规划约束不足；模型缩放无法解决，而结构知识注入带来一致增益。

Conclusion

手术规划评估应从轨迹相似性转向协议遵循，以协议遵循作为评估标准；结构知识注入比单纯模型缩放更有效。

Abstract

Large-scale digital repositories with million plus digitized volumes serve as vital engines for scientific and scholarly discovery. However, as collections grow, finding specific research or educational material remains a significant challenge. This difficulty is compounded by the multimodal nature of texts, that is, texts which combine dense narrative content with essential illustrative charts, annotated diagrams, and complex layouts. Historically, discovery has been supported by full-text search indexes implemented as inverted indexes (e.g., Lucene or Solr). While these systems scale well for textual strings, they are fundamentally "blind" to the semantic nuance of visual information. We explore the benefit of discovery through texts that have been converted to numerical vectors. By representing page images as numerical vectors rather than OCR-extracted text, and doing so for both text and images on a page, we capture both textual and visual semantics while mitigating reverse engineering and copyright infringement concerns. The primary contribution of this research is an evaluation of the multimodal retrieval landscape, encompassing both semantic precision and architectural scalability. Our findings demonstrate that integrating visual and textual features significantly enhances the discovery of relevant material, particularly in domains where diagrammatic content is essential for conceptual understanding. We assess retrieval efficacy, demonstrating that the system achieves sub-linear scalability. Our findings suggest that an optimized vector database configuration is the critical factor in transforming VLM-based embeddings into a production-ready, library-scale discovery solution.

Motivation

大规模数字馆藏中，传统倒排索引全文检索对包含图表、注释和复杂版式的多模态文本语义理解不足，且OCR文本存在逆向工程与版权风险，需要探索基于数值向量的多模态发现方法。

We release this entire dataset, and our custom annotation tools will also be made available, in order to allow for ongoing expansion. We also provide a series of benchmarks for the tasks of Instance Segmentation, Pose detection, Temporal Action Localisation, and finally video-text retrieval. The totality of this multi-modal dataset is the data needed to solve both these individual computer vision tasks as well as enabling more complex multi-modal tasks making use of a combination of annotations.

Motivation

动物计算机视觉数据集在计算机视觉和动物行为研究中日益重要，有助于通过多种视觉任务理解复杂场景，从而改善动物健康与福利。现有数据集缺乏多模态标注的整合，因此需要构建一个综合性的多模态猫理解数据集。

Method

基于Cat Royale艺术与研究项目中超过500小时的视频，构建了CRCD数据集，包含20,000张带实例分割掩码的图像、5,000张带多主体姿态坐标的图像、800多个带动作类别的视频片段和800多个带详细文本描述的视频片段。发布了数据集、自定义标注工具，并提供了实例分割、姿态检测、时序动作定位和视频-文本检索任务的基准测试。

Result

发布了CRCD数据集及其标注工具，提供了四个计算机视觉任务的基准测试，并展示了该数据集支持个体任务和复杂多模态任务的能力。

Conclusion

CRCD是首个将多种标注类型链接在一起用于多模态使用的猫数据集，其完整数据既能解决单个计算机视觉任务，也能支持结合多种标注的复杂多模态任务，并可通过公开工具持续扩展。

Abstract

Data-driven approaches like deep learning are rapidly advancing planetary science, particularly in Mars exploration. Despite recent progress, most existing benchmarks remain confined to closed-set supervised visual tasks and do not support text-guided retrieval for geospatial discovery. We introduce MarsRetrieval, a retrieval benchmark for evaluating vision-language models for Martian geospatial discovery. MarsRetrieval includes three tasks: (1) paired image-text retrieval, (2) landform retrieval, and (3) global geo-localization, covering multiple spatial scales and diverse geomorphic origins. We propose a unified retrieval-centric protocol to benchmark multimodal embedding architectures, including contrastive dual-tower encoders and generative vision-language models. Our evaluation shows MarsRetrieval is challenging: even strong foundation models often fail to capture domain-specific geomorphic distinctions. We further show that domain-specific fine-tuning is critical for generalizable geospatial discovery in planetary settings. Our code is available at https://github.com/ml-stat-Sustech/MarsRetrieval

Motivation

现有火星探索基准局限于封闭集监督视觉任务，不支持文本引导的检索用于地理空间发现，缺乏对视觉语言模型在行星科学中检索能力的评估。

Method

构建MarsRetrieval检索基准，涵盖三个任务（配对图文检索、地貌检索、全局地理定位），覆盖多空间尺度和多样地貌起源；提出统一的以检索为中心的协议来基准测试多模态嵌入架构，包括对比双塔编码器和生成式视觉语言模型。

Result

评估显示MarsRetrieval具有挑战性：即使强大的基础模型也常无法捕捉领域特定地貌区分；领域特定微调对于行星环境中可泛化的地理空间发现至关重要。

Conclusion

MarsRetrieval为火星地理空间发现提供了检索基准，揭示了现有模型的不足，并强调领域微调的必要性，代码已开源。
